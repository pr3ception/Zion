# Energy Systems and Loginoids

This log summarizes the notable mechanisms explored so far.

## Beadron coil array

A 0.325" neosphere jumping between wrapped coils can generate about 40 mV per jump. The beadron concept continues to prove itself as a simple loginoid capable of producing usable energy.

## Hematite vibration

Dual hematite spheres around 0.92" produce a characteristic "zoink" sound when tapped, coinciding with a measurable energy increase. These observations suggest vibergy capture is possible from vibrational resonance.

## Spheron fan test

A pair of small fans positioned face to face will lift light objects between them. Placing glass pockets with spherical beads near taped coils caused the beads to rotate in a tight vortex while floating. The setup acts like a buoyant torandic elevator, hinting that combining air flow with magnetic lift may produce significant "megorce" when tuned.

## Combined simulation

The `Sim.py` demo shows a coil-bead test, a dual-turbine model and a vibergy calculation example. Run `python3 Sim.py` to see these in action.

## 168-state pattern

`temple.py` prints a basic outline of the 168-state ziggunaut layout described in `Dovortaxs.txt`. This provides a template for further study of the full system.
